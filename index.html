About

Building Beyond The Interface.

I'm Muhammad Hammad, a full-stack developer. I work on both sides of a website: the responsive interface people see, and the PHP, .NET and MySQL systems that make it actually do something.

Most of my projects start with the data. Once I know what needs to be stored and how people will find it, I design the pages around it, then add interaction where it helps someone get a task done faster. Lately I have also been pushing plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript as far as they go: 3D scenes, scroll stories, audio and generative art.
